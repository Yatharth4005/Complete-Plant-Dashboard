💡 Governance Roles & Responsibilities

Detailed protocols and execution requirements for each governance level.

👑 Project Sponsor
Executive Director (Unit Head — JSP Raigarh++)
  • Ensure the TPM initiative aligns with the organization's overall strategic goals and objectives.
  • Resource Allocation and key Decision Making for the TPM implementation.
👥 Steering Committee
Chairman, Vice Chairman & Board Members
  • Approve TPM implementation roadmap.
  • Conduct monthly TPM progress review.
  • Recommend teams for reward & recognition.
  • Arrange for recognition and felicitation for teams achieving TPM quarterly targets.
⚙️ TPM Coordinators
Raj Bhushan (Pillar) & Raunika (Workstation/Cell)
  • Pillar (Raj Bhushan): Prepare and manage the training calendar, release monthly reports, sign off milestone stages, and audit documentation (OPL/Standards).
  • Logistics (Raunika): Maintain trainee attendance, coordinate assessments, and manage training organization scheduling, travel, accommodation & bills.
🏢 HOD / Area Owners
Department Heads & Zonal Heads
  • Identify cells and workstations along with their team members.
  • Review and approve quarterly targets of workstations and cells.
  • Ensure participation & attendance of teams in TPM related trainings.
  • Ensure active participation of the Cell and Workstation teams during reviews.
  • Provide necessary resources (e.g. tools, materials) and conduct fortnightly reviews.
🤝 Cell Leaders
Cell Team Heads
  • Provide necessary technical guidance to the workstation teams.
  • Address any cross-functional coordination and alignment issues.
  • Conduct weekly progress reviews for cell workstations.
🔧 Workstation Leads
Workstation SPOC / Lead Operators
  • Achieve quarterly goals of KPIs.
  • Ensure timely actions on identified abnormalities.
  • Lead Zero leakage drives and assign tasks to team members.
  • Ensure presence of team and timely action on review feedbacks.
🛠️ Workstation / Cell Teams
Ground workforce execution teams
  • Identify abnormalities / failures and address them including analysis, RCA with permanent solutions.
  • Complete the allocated tasks on time.
  • Participate in internal & external reviews.
  • Maintain 5S in Workstations.
